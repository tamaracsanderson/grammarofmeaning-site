What happens when we balance the corpus?

Three beats. (1) The problem: the corpus is 85% Abrahamic. (2) The test: a tradition-separation score — does each passage sit closer to its own tradition than to any other? — reads positive on the raw corpus, as if traditions clump into their own territories; so we cap every family to the same size and re-measure. (3) The finding: the score drops through zero into interleave — the apparent separation was the big Abrahamic bloc looking tight next to a sparse rest, not a stable property of the space. In short: the separation is prevalence-sensitive. (Metric: tradition-label silhouette. "Near ≠ same doctrine" — near = similar coded stance.)
