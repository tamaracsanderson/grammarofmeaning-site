The symmetries and returns

Look for the form, not the flow. Where does the passage echo, pair, fold back on itself, or leap? Shape is not a chain — it's the architecture the moves make together.

Each structure gets its own geometry, not a colour: a ring is a concentric arc around a marked pivot; a parallel is a paired bracket; a juxtaposition is a held-open seam (no line — the withheld connection is the point); a leap is a broken jump.
