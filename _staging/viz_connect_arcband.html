The Arc Band

The moves stay in reading order; an arc joins two that are linked. The shape of the passage is the shape of the arcs.

Each row is a move, top to bottom in textual order. An arc in the left margin joins two linked moves; its colour is the kind of link, and it lands on the move it points to. The faint dotted arcs are plain sequence (“and then”) — the default; the coloured arcs are the ones that carry meaning. Click a legend chip to isolate one kind.
