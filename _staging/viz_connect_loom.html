The Relation Loom

The same links as the Arc Band, woven as a grid — every move against every other, so clusters and hubs show at a glance.

Each row is a move that reaches out; each column a move reached to. A woven cell means a link runs from that row-move to that column-move — its colour is the kind of link. Near the diagonal = neighbours holding together; far off it = a long reach across the passage. Click a legend chip to isolate a kind; hover a cell for the pair.
