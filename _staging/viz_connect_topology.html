The Topology Lab

The same links, but as a graph — reading order dropped, so hubs, clusters, and isolated moves show by position.

Each dot is a move; bigger dots are hubs (more links). A line is a link, coloured by kind. Moves that link tightly pull together into clusters; a move with no links drifts to the edge. This deliberately sets textual order aside — it’s the research view, not the reading. Click a legend chip to isolate a kind; hover a dot for its move + links.
