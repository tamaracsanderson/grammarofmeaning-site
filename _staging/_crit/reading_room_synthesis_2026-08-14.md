# Reading Room — the synthesis (the architecture the whole crit-walk resolves into)

**Figure:** `_staging/viz_reading_sequence.html` → becomes **the Reading Room**. · **PI + GPT crit 2026-08-14.**
**This is not one more figure-fix — it's the unifying architecture.** Every figure reviewed this session becomes a lens or card inside ONE reading environment.

## The core move: the TEXT is the sequence, not the layers
Retire "layers as a reading sequence" (`draw → linger → respond → reflect`). The current page makes the *analytical layers* the sequence; instead **the passage itself is the sequence, and the analytical world appears beside it as you read.** Potential jumps to 5/5 across the board. The formulation: **"The text is the road; everything else belongs in the landscape beside it."** A **digital critical edition** (main text · marginal scholia · apparatus · figures) with modern behaviors (hover/filter/sync/pin/expand/search/audio) — NOT a dashboard of analysis outputs.

## The architecture (three columns; ~18–20% / 52–58% / 24–28%)
- **CENTER = the Move Score** (the permanent, immutable spine — verbatim verse primary, move paraphrases beneath, nesting visible). Do NOT build another Mark representation. **The page must still be a beautiful reading experience with all margins OFF** (the usability test).
- **LEFT = what changes INSIDE the text** (structural, graphical, sparse). Position as a **shift/braid rail** — DNA/subway showing **changes from baseline, not eight values × 55 moves**; hover a move → all 8 coordinates + "changed from prev: epistemic warrant testimony→observation". One neutral baseline, only the *changed* coordinate surfaces (not an 8-color Christmas tree).
- **RIGHT = the world AROUND the text** (contextual, prose+image, anchored to the exact move where it becomes active).

## ⭐ THE SYNTHESIS — every figure becomes a lens/card here
This is what the whole walk was converging on:
- **CENTER** = Move Score (the one the PI said "looks good").
- **LEFT LENSES** (one at a time; center never changes): **Position** (braid) · **Logic** (the arrows/connectives view) · **Shape** (the brackets) · **Sound**. → *the Connect views (Logic/Shape/Sound) stop being destination pages and become margin lenses on the spine.* This is the big IA simplification, and it reconciles with the "5 peer instruments" Connect IA: they're lenses on one reading, not five separate tools.
- **RIGHT CARDS** (reader-facing tabs, engine names inside): **WORLD** (Sitz + Paradigm) · **INHERITS** (Lineage — the watershed/matrix, anchored) · **AFTERLIVES** (Reception + Gloss) · **OPEN** (Gap / Seam — the Ledger). Anchored to the move that makes them relevant (M7 stone → Sitz tomb-sealing; M13 → Lineage Isaiah 53; M23 → the ending's gap).
- **Position ↔ Morphospace/Neighbors** — a selected move's left-rail coordinates tie to "see nearest neighbors" (the Atlas).

So Trace/Scan/Map/Logic/Shape, Lineage matrix/watershed, Sitz/Paradigm, the Ledger/Facing-Page, Position/Morphospace/Neighbors — **all of it lives in the margins of the Move Score.** The crit-walk's recurring contract (show the real text · detail beside the field · explain+interpret) IS this design.

## Interaction (from the crit)
- **Scroll is the primary synchronizer** + a subtle **focus line** (~40% down) → the move crossing it is "active"; margins track it (prev/next cards at 25% opacity for continuity — no hard disappear).
- **Pin** a move (FOLLOW ↔ PINNED) so a card doesn't vanish on scroll.
- **Progressive disclosure:** LEVEL 1 margin mark (S/P/L/R/G) → LEVEL 2 glance card → LEVEL 3 click = full evidence/citations/coding.
- **Depth control** (replaces the linear ritual): `Text · Moves · Annotated · Research` (a viewing preference, not a journey). **Margin density:** `Quiet · Annotated · Full`.
- **Audio read-aloud** — as it plays, verse highlights, active move shifts, Position rail moves, cards update (the "sequence" comes from the text/audio itself). **Guided mode** optional (a tour through the environment, not the environment).
- **Whitespace is load-bearing** — annotations are *episodic*; the silence between them is part of the reading. Avoid the "tunnel" (busy both margins choking the text).
- **Responsive as a data model:** annotation = {anchor move, family, priority, compact, expanded} → desktop dual-margin, tablet one flippable margin, mobile inline-after-move. Don't hardcode left/right.
- **Rename:** retire "The Reading Sequence" → **"Read"** / **"Reading Room"** ("Stay with the text; let its structure and world appear around it").

## Ownership
- **Mine (render):** the Reading Room shell — Move Score spine, the two margins, scroll-sync + focus line, pin, progressive disclosure, depth/density controls, the lens switcher, reader-facing labels. Most of this is Design.
- **Atlas/Method (data):** the Position per-move shift data (which coordinate changes where) + the anchored cards' content (Sitz/Paradigm/Lineage/Reception/Gap per move). Same substrate as Neighbors + the node drawers.
