Four stances a text can take toward its tradition — and who shares them

Two questions locate every coded observation: does it receive its tradition or interrogate it? does it seek belonging or rupture? That makes four corners — four archetypes. Color marks the tradition. The finding is in the mixing: each archetype is shared by 6–16 traditions that never met — so the archetype, not the religion, is the deeper grouping.
