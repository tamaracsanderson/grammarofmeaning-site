Do traditions cluster by tradition — or interleave?

Each dot is one observation a text makes about reality — its stance on eight questions we code (what it treats as real, how it knows, what it's for, and so on). Two dots sit close when the two observations make similar moves, far apart when they differ. Color marks the tradition. So the question is plain — do the colors clump into separate territories, or blend?

the fine print — how the space is built, and the honest numbers ▾

The space is a computed compression (MCA — correspondence analysis) of the eight coded axes: it finds the directions along which the observations differ most, so that nearness on the plot means agreement across those eight axes. The axes aren't labels to decode — they're just those directions of difference.
