The Gospels · coded moves

The frame, as it shifts

Each account read as its coded moves — and, move by move, the frame it is working in. The account has one overall frame; but the frame is not flat. It veers, move to move — and where it veers is the signal.

A veer = a move whose frame-axis value differs from the account's baseline. This is one coder, one model, one pass — a reference read, not a measured-reliability one.

Versions — the coded moves were done on the NRSV (the analytical edition); the reading room displays KJV (the four accounts, whole) and WEB (the braided reading + 1 Cor 15). Recording the edition into per-move provenance is a pending backfill.
