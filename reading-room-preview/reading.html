The Gospels · reading as moves

Read the account, watch the frame shift

Each gospel read as its coded moves, in order. As you scroll, the rail on the right holds the frame the current move is working in — and lights up wherever it veers from the account's baseline.

One coder, one pass — a reference read, not a measured-reliability one. A move is one thing the text does — a single operation (announce, negate, identify) with its arguments; it's smaller than a verse, and a verse usually holds two or three. Each move carries its scripture in the WEB (the World English Bible, a public-domain modern translation), aligned by verse, plus its full 7-part grammar (narrator · agent/operation/substrate/outcome · voice/type). All four gospels now cover the whole resurrection chapter.
