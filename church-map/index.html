A red→green overlay of how thoroughly each area is mapped — so you can tell "few churches" apart from "few mapped churches."
A dark or empty-looking area usually means OpenStreetMap volunteers haven’t mapped it in detail yet — not that it has few religious places. OSM’s categories are richest for Christian denominations and thinner elsewhere, so places like the Sahel & Horn of Africa look empty when they aren’t. Turn on the completeness layer to see where the map is thorough (green) vs thin (red).
