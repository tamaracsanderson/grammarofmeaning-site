What a text is made of

A schema of the instrument. What it is for, and what it claims →  ·  Objections & answers →  ·  R&D: gaps in a foundational paper →

One text goes in. We situate it — where it stands, the world it came from — and take it apart into the moves it's built from: what it says, and what it leaves unsaid. Then we recompose those moves several ways — as the alternatives it could have been, as the same text under a changed world, as commentary on its silences, as the readings history gave it, as its variants across tellings, and as a non-linear field of its own recurrences. Each recomposition changes what it means.
