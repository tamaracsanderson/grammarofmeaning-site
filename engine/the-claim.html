Grammar of Meaning

Why this, not just an LLM

Ask an LLM about grace and you get a reading — one opaque answer you can only nod at or reject whole. You can't reproduce it, you can't check it, you can't say where it's wrong.

This project builds a telescope, not a conversation. It breaks a text into the moves it's actually making, scans for what it leaves unsaid, and recomposes those moves into the meanings it could carry — and every one of those is checkable at a named move. The LLM isn't the oracle; it's a lens inside the instrument — many trained perspectives on one text at once, disciplined by a method anyone can run, reproduce, and disagree with specifically.

The point was never the AI's opinion. It's a reproducible apparatus for seeing how meaning gets made — and published, so others can extend and contest it. (Smil withholds his method; this one ships it.)

The ah-ha's

1 · Absence becomes an object. The strangest move — and the one the critic panel flagged as maximum novelty, the move no other critic named: a negation — what a text does not say — is made into a positive, coded, queryable entity. Meaning lives partly in the unsaid; this makes the unsaid sayable.

2 · Two kinds of silence. A gap is an in-text silence — the text goes quiet on its own question. A seam is a silence between the text and its context or version — a translator's forced choice, a cut passage, which edition you hold. Different objects, kept apart.

3 · The method turns on itself. Run on its own method-statement, the instrument found a commitment it had left unsaid — and let its own forebears (Genette, Kugel) adjudicate it. It can find, name, and have its lineage judge its own silences.

4 · Structure sees what intuition misses. On Romans, the disciplined per-move scan caught the most theologically-loaded silence — why God justifies — that a strong, unstructured reading walked straight past.

5 · The reader is a system. Meaning is made in the reading — and here the reader is a hybrid: a person and an AI, each bringing a frame. Naming that reader is part of the method, not a disclaimer.

6 · Framing is load-bearing. The same question under two framings gives opposite answers. This makes position-dependence auditable instead of hidden.
