Method & Instrument

The engine reads across two bands. Above: the grammar — the domain-agnostic instrument that can be pointed at any corpus (what a move is, how the frame shifts, where texts rhyme). Below: the Christian canon — the proof-of-concept domain's own voice (its reception, its lineage, its witnesses). The grammar transfers; the canon is this domain's content.

Click any output for its drawer. Every drawer renders from live data — an output with no data export yet shows as a dimmed awaiting-export card, so the page can't hide un-built work (that honesty is the point).
