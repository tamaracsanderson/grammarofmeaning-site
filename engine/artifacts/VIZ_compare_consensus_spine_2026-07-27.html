Reading the icons

One neutral hue throughout — the state is carried by the mark and its position, never by colour. “Omitted” is a lighter grey only to recede, not to read as worse.

The move survives; its grammar of agency bends

A control-variable comparison: the same content, the translation varied across the Greek → Latin → English stream. At three loci the underlying move is conserved, but who does it, to whom, and under what condition drifts. No edition is the baseline — the invariant move is the spine; the drift is what the icons mark.
