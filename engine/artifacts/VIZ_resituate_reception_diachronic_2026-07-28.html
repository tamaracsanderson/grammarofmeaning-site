Perturbation predicts; reception confirms

The Nicene test-word across 325 → 341 → 381 · RESITUATE → reception history

RESITUATE asked a counterfactual: strip the emperor from Nicaea 325, and what happens to the creed? It predicted the precise test-word (homoousios) and the anathemas are the volatile shell — contingent on institutional power, not on theology. Here is that prediction tested against what the creed actually did over the next 56 years. The counterfactual told us where to look; the real revision history confirms the direction.

Institutional power backing the Nicene line (documented judgment): high under Constantine, ebbs under Constantius, returns under Theodosius.

325
homoousios present
“… being of one substance (homoousion, consubstantialem) with the Father” — plus the anathemas that curse and cast out the rival view.
The precise, non-scriptural test-word is imposed and the rival view is anathematized.
Power: Constantine convenes + funds the council and enforces its result (exiles dissenters).
341
homoousios absent
“God from God, whole from whole, sole from sole, perfect from perfect … exact Image of the Godhead” — scriptural language that deliberately avoids ‘one substance.’
With enforcement gone, the test-word drops and the anathema block is replaced by looser ‘three in subsistence’ language — exactly the low-enforcement outcome CF1 predicted.
Power: Under Constantius II the anti-Nicene party is ascendant; no power compels the Nicene line.
381
homoousios restored + expanded
“consubstantial with the Father” restored — plus “whose kingdom shall have no end” (vs Marcellus) and the expanded Spirit clause (“the Lord and Giver of life”).
Power returns; the test-word returns AND expands to answer the new live enemies.
Power: Theodosius makes Nicene Christianity the state religion (380) and convenes the council.
The finding. The test-word tracks institutional power up → down → up. It is present when an emperor can compel (325), drops the moment enforcement lifts (341, Antioch — the creed keeps scriptural language and deliberately avoids ‘one substance’), and returns and expands when power comes back (381). That is exactly the fork RESITUATE's CF1 perturbation surfaced — run forward on real history. The parts of the creed that feel most essential (the exact homoousios, the curses) are the most historically contingent; the narrative economy is the stable invariant. Perturbation predicted the pressure-point; reception history confirmed it.
