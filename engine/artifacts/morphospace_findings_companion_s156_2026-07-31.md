# What the map is (in one breath)
Every reading is coded on **8 categorical axes** — its *frame profile*. **MCA** (the categorical cousin of PCA) turns those
profiles into map coordinates: readings with similar profiles sit near each other. The two drawn axes are the **two biggest
composite contrasts** across the 8 (not 2 of the 8), holding **41.9%** of the structure in 2D. It's an **orientation
device**, not the whole picture — meaning-space is genuinely high-dimensional (below). *(Full: `FAQ_comprehension_pca_mca`.)*

# 1 · The four quadrants = SOURCE × POSTURE
The two contrasts have clean semantics — and they read straight off the quadrants:
- **Dim 1 = the SOURCE / ground** — *observed·immanent-material* ↔ *revealed·transcendent-soteriological*.
- **Dim 2 = the POSTURE toward it** — *hold / dwell in it* ↔ *act on / unsettle it*.

So four stances of meaning-making fall out: **descriptive-integrative** (observe + integrate — systems science) ·
**critical-materialist** (observe + rupture — critique/deconstruction) · **devotional-sacramental** (revealed + hold —
liturgy/devotion) · **prophetic-transformative** (revealed + rupture — prophetic/liberationist). The tell that they're
*real*: **P-T is "active D-S"** (same revealed source, opposite posture), and the same hold-vs-rupture split repeats on the
observed side. **Emergent, not imposed** — the geometry found them, and they map onto real families unprompted.
*(Full: `morphospace_quadrant_typology_finding`.)*

# 2 · Who's actually in each box (+ the density)
| quadrant | n (%) | who lands there |
|---|---|---|
| descriptive-integrative | 299 (33%) | biggest + most family-diverse — philosophy, psychology, empirical academic work |
| critical-materialist | 267 (29%) | scholarship *about* traditions; most Indigenous-heavy |
| devotional-sacramental | 197 (22%) | primary scripture in devotional register — Dharmic 36% + Abrahamic 31% |
| prophetic-transformative | 148 (16%) | smallest; committed/world-transforming |

**Density is LEFT-heavy** (observed = 62%; descriptive-integrative is the densest single box). The revealed right is
sparser — partly §2.8 corpus-skew, partly real. *(Full: `morphospace_2x2_population_and_sensecheck`.)*

# 3 · The map sense-checks the coder (§2.8 working)
Revealed-religion texts landing in **critical-materialist** (80 reads) is *mostly expected*: they're scholarship *about*
the tradition (critical voice), correctly tagged but not devotional — the map **distinguishes "scripture" from
"scholarship about scripture."** The instrument validates itself. A handful of *actual primary scripture* carrying
`reductive-deconstructive` codes are **coding-review candidates** (judgment withheld): Young's-Literal-Romans (our seed
text — worth a look), Vulgate-Acts, SBLGNT-John, and the Aggañña Sutta (likely a genuine finding — it *is* satirical).
**Key distinction:** the map reads **stance, not tradition-membership.**

# 4 · Meaning-space is high-dimensional (a finding, not a flaw)
There's **no clean 80/20**: by the average-inertia rule, **51 dimensions carry more than their fair share.** You stop at
the elbow (2-3D) for the *map*, knowing it's an orientation device — but the long tail is itself the finding:
**meaning-making doesn't collapse to a few axes.**

# 5 · The anomalies (the held paradoxes)
The rarest cross-axis combo: **`reverent` × `reductive-deconstructive`** — expected ~28, observed just 2 (Jung's
*Psychology of the Unconscious* + the Tanya): *reverent dismantling.* Reverence and deconstruction almost never travel
together — which is exactly the held-paradox the Divergence chair exists to surface. **(And note: this instrument's own
stance is `reverent × reductive-deconstructive` — we revere texts and take them apart to understand them. The project
lives in the anomaly quadrant.)**

# An honest limit
The 8 axes code the **observable/stated stance** — the **unconscious is not an axis.** So the map (like a
synthetic-respondent model) reads the reportable surface, not the generator beneath it. A candidate depth-layer, and a
genuine reflexive limit worth naming.
