# TL;DR

Run through the method's three gates — **recurrence · orthogonality · coherence** — the
eschatological-temporality proposal **does not clear the bar for a 9th axis**. It is the single
most-repeated new-axis proposal in the 795 (`eschatological-temporality` ×3), but that is near-singleton;
its reads are **strongly correlated** with existing axis values (revelation, performative,
transcendent-personal, salvation) rather than spread independently; and the "temporality" label **lumps
several distinct things**. **VERDICT: BUNDLE** — primary home **telos** (the temporal mode/inflection of
salvation & justice-world-transformation), with **epistemic-warrant=revelation** the strongest single
correlate. This matches the reader's OWN disposition (it filed 5/14 under telos and only 3/14 as truly
`separate`). One honest caveat drives the recommendation to keep a small **DEFER** queue rather than a
hard DROP: the orthogonality "fail" is **partly confounded with corpus skew** (13/14 cluster reads are
Abrahamic), and n=14 is small.

# 1. The cluster (Step 1 — identify, don't pre-decide)

Two nested definitions, both queried live from `atlas_frame_read_candidate` (all `pending`):

| Cluster | Match rule | Candidates | Distinct reads in the 911 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CORE** — location of meaning in cosmic/historical time | `proposed_value ~ eschat\|protolog\|yugic` | **14** | 14 |
| **BROAD** — anything "temporal"-named | adds `temporal\|diachron\|time-horizon\|…` | 31 | 31 |

**Recurrence is low.** CORE = 14 of 795 candidates (**1.8%**) on 14 of 911 reads (**1.5%**). The most-repeated
*exact* proposal string is `eschatological-temporality` at **3×**; every other member is a 1–2× near-singleton
(`eschatological-horizon` ×2, `protological-genealogy-of-evil` ×2, `yugic-temporality` ×1, etc.). For scale:
the reader tagged nearly all 774 new-axis candidates with a self-disposition, and only **39 (~5%)** are flagged
`separate` (a genuine "needs a new axis"). The reader's own default is already to bundle.

# 2. The reader already disposed of most of it (a strong prior on the call)

Each candidate's `rationale` carries the reader's own `[relation=…]` tag. For the CORE 14:

| Reader's disposition | Count | Which |
|---|---|---|
| `folds_under:telos` | **5** | eschatological-accountability, -horizon(this-world/Hereafter), -restoration, -temporal-horizon, -time-horizon |
| `separate` | **3** | `eschatological-temporality` ×3 (Acts 2:17/Joel; Matt 13:30 wheat&weeds; Matt 19:28 παλιγγενεσία) |
| `expands:r_topology` | 2 | protological-genealogy-of-evil ×2 (John 8:44 "ἀνθρωποκτόνος ἀπ' ἀρχῆς") |
| `expands:ontological-commitment` | 1 | yugic-temporality (Kali-Yuga cycle) |
| `folds_under:r_topology` | 1 | prophetic-eschatological-temporality |
| `expands:epistemic-warrant` | 1 | eschatological-ripeness (kairos-readiness as divine trigger) |
| `(no tag)` | 1 | eschatological-horizon |

The reader's judgment is itself the coherence finding: the "temporality" candidates **do not point at one
axis** — they fold/expand across **four** different existing axes. That is not the signature of one clean
orthogonal dimension; it is the signature of a family of temporal *inflections* on several existing axes.

# 3. Orthogonality (Step 2 — the empirical test)

For each of the 8 axes, Cramér's V between "is-in-CORE-cluster (y/n)" and the axis value, over the 911 reads
(n_cluster=14). **If temporality were orthogonal, the cluster would spread like the full corpus and every V
would sit near 0.** It does not:

| Axis | ChiSq | Cramér's V | Dominant cluster value (cluster share \| full-corpus share) |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **epistemic-warrant** | 138.7 | **0.390** | revelation **86%** \| 7% |
| **telos** | 70.6 | **0.278** | salvation **43%** \| 4%  · +justice-world-transformation 29% \| 10% = **72% \| 14%** |
| **ontological-commitment** | 64.0 | **0.265** | transcendent-personal **57%** \| 9% |
| **hermeneutic-posture** | 48.2 | **0.230** | performative **86%** \| 17% |
| **r_topology** | 41.4 | 0.213 | graded-asymmetry 21% \| 6% |
| **r_condition** | 39.1 | 0.207 | covenantal 29% \| 5% |
| **evaluative-stance** | 26.0 | 0.169 | reverent **57%** \| 15% |
| **inferential-operation** | 15.8 | 0.132 | causal-explanation 36% \| 24% |

**Reading.** The cluster is a tightly-correlated *corner* of the existing space, not an independent axis. It
is, almost exactly, the "**Abrahamic-canonical**" combo the field-usage analysis already named as the
strongest religious shape (revelation · transcendent-personal · salvation · reverent · performative). The
strongest correlate is **epistemic-warrant=revelation** (V=0.390); the reader's nominated home,
**telos**, shows the second-strongest and most legible lift: salvation jumps from a 4% corpus share to 43%
inside the cluster (an ~11× lift), and salvation+justice-world-transformation together cover 72% of the
cluster vs 14% of the corpus.

Per the method's own rule — *"if a cluster's distinctions correlate with an existing axis → it is not
orthogonal → bundle"* — this **fails the orthogonality gate**. It does not spread across the 8; it piles onto
a few of their values.

The 3 `separate`-tagged reads alone (the tightest possible core) do **not** rescue the case: over n=3 they
are **100% revelation, 100% transcendent-personal, 100% performative** — i.e. even the reads the reader
thought were orthogonal are perfectly predicted by three existing axes. Their genuine residue is narrower
than "eschatology": it is the *structure of historical time* (linear-toward-terminus vs cyclic vs mythic-now
vs eternal-present), which telos does not encode. That residue is real but rare and confounded (see §5).

# 4. Coherence (Step 3 gate — one dimension or several lumped?)

**Partial, and it degrades as you widen the net.**
- The CORE reads share a loose theme (meaning indexed to a point in cosmic time) but split internally across
  four host axes (§2), so they are not one clean dimension.
- The BROAD "temporal-*" set is clearly **heterogeneous** — it lumps at least four unrelated moves:
  *end-time horizon* (telos), *diachronic causation / deep-time pathogenesis* (an inferential-operation
  facet), *temporal legitimacy — antiquity-as-authority* (an epistemic-warrant/institutional facet), and
  *retrospective-authorial stance / historiographical verdict* (a hermeneutic-posture facet). Its disposition
  tally (7 folds_under:telos, 6 separate, 6 expands:r_topology, 5 folds_under:r_topology, …) confirms the
  lumping. Treating "temporality" as one axis would merge things that belong to at least three existing axes.

# 5. Honest caveat — the fail is partly confounded (§2.8, §13)

The orthogonality result cannot be taken fully at face value:

- **Corpus skew.** 13/14 cluster reads are **Abrahamic** (11 Abrahamic + 2 ABR); only 1 is Dharmic (the
  lone yugic read). Because our coded eschatological material is almost entirely Abrahamic canon, "temporality
  co-loads with revelation/transcendent-personal/salvation" is **partly an artifact of which eschatological
  texts we have coded**, not proof that temporality *is* telos. A corpus with more non-Abrahamic
  temporal material (Hindu yuga, Buddhist kalpa, Norse/Aztec cyclical time, Zoroastrian frashokereti) could
  decorrelate temporality from revelation — the single yugic read is exactly the kind of case that would.
- **Small n.** 14 reads (3 in the tightest core) is too few for the Cramér's V to be more than directional.

So the data **can** decide the immediate question (on current evidence, temporality is not an orthogonal 9th
axis) but **cannot** cleanly decide the deep question (is *structure-of-historical-time* orthogonal in
principle?). That is why the recommendation is BUNDLE-now **+ a scoped DEFER**, not a hard DROP.

# 6. Verdict (Step 4 inputs for the CLC walk)

**BUNDLE** — eschatological-temporality does **not** graduate to a 9th axis.

- **Gate 1 recurrence — FAIL** (14/795 = 1.8%; top exact repeat 3×; near-singleton long tail).
- **Gate 2 orthogonality — FAIL** (strongly correlated with revelation/telos/transcendent-personal/performative;
  every axis V ≥ 0.13, four ≥ 0.23; the cluster is the Abrahamic-canonical corner, not an independent spread).
- **Gate 3 coherence — PARTIAL** (the label lumps ≥3 distinct moves; the tight core still splits across 4 host axes).

**Disposition to carry into the CLC:**
1. **Bundle the eschatological/judgment reads under `telos`** as a temporal *mode/inflection* of
   `salvation` and `justice-world-transformation` — exactly the reader's majority call (folds_under:telos ×5).
   Optionally record a `telos` **new-VALUE/modifier** such as `salvation:eschatological-horizon` if the atlas
   wants to tag the temporal framing without a new axis.
2. **DEFER (not drop) the narrow residue** — *structure of historical time* (linear / cyclic / mythic-now /
   eternal-present), evidenced by the 3 `separate` reads + the yugic read. Log it as a **candidate new-value
   / cross-axis modifier**, reason: rare (4 reads) + confounded (93% Abrahamic). **Re-run this exact test**
   after more non-Abrahamic / cyclical-time eschatology is coded; promote only if it then decorrelates from
   revelation AND recurs.
3. The protological pair and yugic read are already better-housed by their reader-nominated expansions
   (`r_topology:protological-source`; `ontological-commitment:cosmological-temporality`) — treat as
   **new-value** candidates on those axes, not as evidence for a temporality axis.

**Method note (the demo worked).** This single test exercised all three gates on real DB numbers and reached
a clean BUNDLE with a principled DEFER — showing the candidate-graduation method can discriminate
without handing raw candidates to cold critics (§2.1). The strongest new-axis *signal* in the 795 still
bundles; that raises the bar (correctly) for the remaining 793.
