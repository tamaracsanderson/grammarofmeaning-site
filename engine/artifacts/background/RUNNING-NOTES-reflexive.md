# 1. The reflexive meta-output — test #1 of many
The pilot is **test #1** of the "reflexive" meta-output, not the finished thing. Run several more rounds. Findings:

# 2. The generative asks (the next reflexive run) — PI-refined
Wire per gap, and per forebear GIVEN the coded object:
1. Comment (what you recognize / what's missing).
2. **Why does this matter to YOUR field** (make them argue its stakes for their discipline).
3. **How would it matter MORE if we did X** (invite extension).
4. **Given the coded object, what would you CREATE / DEVELOP / ANALYZE from it** (generative — new outputs, not defense).
5. **Propose ideas to make this MORE novel** — NOT "is it novel?" (generative, not evaluative — PI lock S156).
6. What OTHER outputs / use-cases can you imagine.
7. UNSAID — what gap does your frame reveal.

# 3. The CRT / ablation test — the "so what" (required for the thesis)
Control = free-form LLM gap-spotting (no method). Treatment = our systematic 6-question gap-scan (the 126/151).
Compare *which gaps each finds* + *what gets filled*. Shows whether the framing + 6 questions surface gaps free-form
doesn't → validates the method itself ("why go through all this work"). Also the A/B the Kugel point implies (does the
coded layer help, or is reading raw as good?). **This belongs in the thesis as the method's validation.**

# 5. Why the group ranked purpose/authority gaps (PI question)
It's BOTH: (a) the personas' disciplines (reader-response/reception/rhetoric care about *whose reading, what authority,
what purpose*) AND (b) the free nomination step (they picked from the 126 the 6 questions produced). The gaps "left on
the table" = the structural/edge ones the method's own interest-metric favored. → A **different cohort (viz/DH/design)
would pick different gaps** — which is exactly why #4 (by-function cohorts) matters.

The gaps ARE questions the method should answer. First-pass answers (reading-SB, from the project — PI reviews/rewrites):

- **The "text" category authority (M4: who says scripture, lyric, methodology are all "text"?)** → the project's

- **The merge-locus (M19→M20: why can parallel tellings merge at the move-graph level?)** → because the **move** (agent/
  operation/substrate/outcome/narrator) is the tradition-neutral unit, more abstract than the words; the consensus spine
  is the LCD shared *move*, never a shared text (§2.1 first-cycle→second-cycle logic).

# 7. Candidate QUDs (the "what is the text answering?" framing) — PI to pick
The pilot used QUD = "What does the instrument do, and what does it claim about how meaning is made?" Alternatives that
steer toward generative ideas / novelty / output-thinking:
1. What could this instrument *become* — what new ways of reading or making does its method invite?
2. Where is this method most novel, and where is it a re-description of existing work?
3. What can be built on top of the coded object that isn't built yet?
4. Whom is this instrument for, and what would each audience need it to do?
5. What does this method assume that it does not defend?
6. If this method is right, what follows — for reading, scholarship, formation?
7. What would this method miss, and what would catch what it misses?
8. What is the smallest change that would most increase what the method can reveal?
9. What genres of output does the structure of a coded text afford?
10. What kind of object is a coded text, and who is its reader?
(Note: a QUD is NOT the Fan's flipped-move — see §9.)

# 8. The reader-is-a-SYSTEM insight (thesis-gold — PI, S156)
From Iser: meaning is produced in the *act of reading*; the coded object is the record of a reading the instrument
performed. So — **the "reader" here is a *system*, not a person with a frame: a system coded by a person (the PI) and an
AI, each carrying a frame.** That IS the recomposition idea — putting the moves back, differently, is the instrument's
*reading*. Belongs on the-claim.html (no "recompose" drawer exists). This reframes reader-response for a person+AI
instrument-reader — a genuine contribution.
