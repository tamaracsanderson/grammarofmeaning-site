# The test (IRR225 U9 — 5/5 warned the 5 canonical texts might over-fit)
Run v3 on a plain, modern, meaning-CLOSING text (the MIT License). A well-calibrated instrument should find FEW gaps here —
most candidates should be `textual_anchor=stated` and get DISCARDED. The discard/survive ratio IS the result.

# The result — the gates worked, decisively
- **12 candidates → ~81% DISCARDED** (13 discarded : 3 survive; effectively **2 clean survivors** + 1 marginal).
- Every `anchor=stated` candidate died on the stated-rule (the "AS IS"/no-liability clauses — explicitly total, so not gaps).
- Every HOOKLESS omission died on the activation/fork gates. **The crisp distinction the whole "unbounded" problem needed:**
  *"silence without a textual hook that a reader forks on is NOT a gap."* The text is silent about governing-law and
  severability — but nothing in its WORDS forks on them, so they're not gaps; a competent reuser reads through them.
- **The 2-3 survivors are EXACTLY the MIT License's genuinely famous silences** — not hallucinated depth:
  1. **Patent-rights scope** (Paradigm·P1, suppression, absent) — forks against "without restriction"; the canonical MIT
     gap, the very reason Apache 2.0 exists. A textual hook + a real fork + high consequence.
  2. **"substantial portions" threshold** (Frame·F2, indeterminacy, implied) — the license's ONLY affirmative obligation,
     and its boundary is undefined; every compliance call routes through it.
  3. (marginal) revocability/perpetuity (Paradigm·P3) — partly self-resolved by "hereby granted."

# Why this matters (it's the property that makes it an INSTRUMENT)
The cohort's central worry was that a bigger taxonomy just generates more plausible-sounding "gaps." This probe shows the
opposite: on a plain text the gates **restrain** the scan to the 2 silences practitioners actually litigate. That restraint
— few, correct gaps on easy text; many, ranked gaps on hard text — is exactly what lets us honestly say **this is an
instrument, not a gap-generator.** The activation + fork + anchor gates (IMP-24/25) are what deliver it: they separate
"the text is silent about X" (not a gap) from "the text's own words leave X open" (a gap).

# Jonah v3 (the hard text) — the gates discarded the RIGHT things, kept the real gaps
The Jonah v3 arm confirms the other half: on a hard, gap-rich text the gates DISCARD precisely the failure-modes the battery
exposed, while keeping the genuine cruxes. Notably, v3 *deliberately entered two probe candidates to test its own gates* and
killed both:
- **C-X1 "Jonah's backstory"** — DISCARDED @ **activation gate**: "an apparatus-manufactured ∅-slot is not a textual silence"
  — a biography cell the grammar admits but the text never opens. **This is the apparatus-manufactured-gap fix (IMP-21) working.**
- **C-X2 "God's character"** — DISCARDED @ **stated-anchor rule**: v2 supplies the Exodus 34:6 mercy-formula in full → stated, not withheld.
- **C-M1 (v1 elided "it")** — DISCARDED @ **non-resolution**: answered within the window by v2.
- **C-S1 (plant species)** — DISCARDED @ **consequence**: the a fortiori lands identically whatever the species.
- **10 gaps KEPT** (passed all 5 gates), each typed+anchored, ranked importance-only, distribution reported separately. Top
  seeds = the real cruxes (death-wish warrant · terminal silence · the a fortiori's ungrounded warrant · "right-hand-from-left"
  denotation · scope via "much livestock").
- **v.5 seam (IMP-18): PARTIAL fix** — v3 *noted* the tension in the move-analysis (M5 "presupposes Nineveh's fate still open,
  against v2") but didn't elevate it to a ranked seed. Better than v2 (which dropped it) but not fully surfaced. Honest → the
  non-slot-silence catch-all (IMP-18) is still owed.

# Net (both arms) — v3 does what IRR225 promised
- **Restraint on plain text:** ~81% discard on the MIT License, 2 clean survivors = the real famous silences.
- **Discipline on hard text:** gates discard apparatus-artifacts + stated + resolved + inconsequential; keep the genuine cruxes,
  typed and anchored and auditable.
That pairing — *few, correct gaps on easy text; many, ranked, typed gaps on hard text, with every discard named* — is exactly
the "instrument not gap-generator" property. The single typed/anchored/gated upgrade delivered it.
