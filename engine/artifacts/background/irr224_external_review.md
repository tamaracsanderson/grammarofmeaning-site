# IRR224 — reconciliation

The control arm of the reflexive test. Five frontier LLMs read the SAME 13-claim method-statement, BLIND (no sight of
our systematic 151 gaps, no scaffold), and gap-spotted free-form. This doc (1) buckets their critiques per Decision-39,
(2) runs the CRT comparison against our systematic scan — **the comparison is the finding** — (3) harvests the
generative suggestions, (4) flags citation veracity per §13.

Headline: the frontier cohort **replicates the in-house 3-agent CRT** and lands its two heaviest blows —
*multi-LLM IRR ≠ validity* and *gap/negative-space is unbounded* — at full **5/5 unanimity**. Both are meta-validity
critiques that our own 6-question systematic scan is **structurally incapable of raising about itself**.

---

## 1. Decision-39 buckets

Cohort key: G=GPT-5 · Gm=Gemini · D=DeepSeek · H=Haiku · S=Solar.

### UNANIMOUS (5/5)

**U1 — Multi-LLM inter-rater reliability ≠ validity; the models are not independent raters.**
All five, independently: LLMs share training corpora / RLHF / benchmark conventions / dominant-language bias, so
agreement measures a *shared prior*, not ground truth. "Shared systemic hallucination or ideological alignment" (Gm);
"statistical house of cards" (D); "measures stability under a model ecology, not reliability across genuinely
independent interpreters" (G); "shared biases aren't independence" (H); "correlated errors… shared training data" (S).
G and S both push further: consensus can *reward the most conventional reading* and suppress the minority
interpretation that is itself the object of study. **This is the sharpest reflexive result of the round — see §2.**

**U2 — Gaps / negative-space is unbounded; gap-scoring has no operational definition.**
All five: "nearly every utterance leaves infinitely many things unstated" (G) — the work is distinguishing a meaningful
gap from an irrelevant absence, which needs a theory of salience / genre / question-under-discussion the method
doesn't supply. "Identifying negative space is infinite; no boundary conditions" (Gm); "unanchored… negative space is
qualitative and relational; quantifying it suggests a cardinality that doesn't exist" (D); "productive silence vs mere
incompleteness" (H); "strategic vs inadvertent omissions" (S). G adds the positive-silence point (O): silence can
*function* — mystery, trauma, esoteric protection — so "gloss fills the gaps" risks treating interpretive openness as
a defect.

**U3 — Edge typology is under-specified; the "…" hides the hardest part of the grammar.**
All five: "presupposes, entails, fills" belong to different theoretical registers (logical / semantic-pragmatic /
method-defined) and the method never gives the complete set or assignment rules. "No formal logic or ontology…
arbitrary narrative glue" (Gm); "the ellipsis hides the hardest part" (D); "exhaustive? mutually exclusive?" (H);
"underlying graph model only hinted at… no rigorous graph-theoretic description" (S).

### STRONG-MAJORITY (4/5)

**SM1 — Domain-generality is asserted, untested, and non-falsifiable; self-application proves consistency, not
generality.** G, D, H, S. "Survival across domains is evidence of transportability, not proof the structure belongs to
the method" (G); "asserted, not argued… running on its own statement is external to the text" (D); "untested… showing
it works on two domains doesn't establish generality" (H); "no empirical test… a rhetorical flourish" (S).
*(Gm out — treats self-application positively as a novelty avenue, the "Ouroboros method.")*

**SM2 — The 5-slot move-grammar is an a-priori ontology that privileges agentive / transitive / propositional / Western
modes.** G, Gm, D, H. "A causal-agentive ontology… may fit narratives, instructions, arguments better than meditations,
koans, prayers, non-agentive metaphysical claims" (G); Gm makes it the cross-check question outright ("prove the
move-grammar does not inherently favor Western, analytic, propositional modes over non-Western, analogical, or
performative"); "the method assumes a Western narrative framework… naively realist" (D); "may privilege narrative,
propositional, agentive over lyric, paradoxical, fragmentary" (H). *(S adjacent — wants more slots for affect/embodiment
but does not frame the grammar as an imposed metaphysics.)* **This is the CRT's deepest critique; the frontier cohort
reproduces it at 4/5.**

**SM3 — No reader / reception / interpreter-positionality; meaning is completed in uptake, and the coder's constitutive
role is invisible.** G, Gm, D, H — and it is the **single most important miss** for three of them. Gm: "embodied,
situated grounding of the human interpreter… a text acts upon a nervous system." D: "total absence of the interpreter's
constitutive role in the coding process itself… identifying a move or a gap is an act of interpretive projection… false
neutrality." H: "the interpreter's stance is rendered invisible… your reading position is a generator of meaning, not
just a reader of it." G (X): models represented meaning more readily than socially enacted meaning. *(S out — its
headline miss is inter-textual resonance, not the reader.)*

**SM4 — Move segmentation / granularity has no principled stopping rule.** G, D, H, S. Is "the kingdom of heaven is like
a mustard seed" one move or three (H)? "How to decide when a move is atomic vs a composition" (S); "the segmentation
axiom is never justified… ambiguity, overlap, simultaneity" (D); one-operation-per-move "may improve reliability while
reducing validity… the grammar risks discovering the discreteness it imposed" (G). *(Gm out.)*

**SM5 — Morphospace axes are under-specified: derivation (a-priori vs induced) and false precision.** G, D, H, S.
"'Each axis evidenced from the text' is tautological… infinitely expandable and non-comparable" (D); "induction vs a
priori… how many axes constitute adequacy?" (H); "a coordinate sounds mathematically exact but a morphospace can
conceal judgment beneath geometry" (G); "different coders could produce radically different coordinates" (S). *(Gm out.)*

**SM6 — The coded object presents a misleading neutrality; it should preserve disagreement / alternative codings /
provenance / versioning.** G, D, H, S. "Better understood as a versioned argument about the text… the store should
preserve disagreement rather than presenting one canonical analysis" (G); "naively realist… treats texts as containers
of retrievable operations" (D); "entangle provenance into the method — don't erase the history of interpretation" (H);
"how conflicts between raters are resolved / version-controlled is undefined" (S). *(Gm out — names the single store of
record a strength.)*

**SM7 — Affect / embodiment / materiality has no slot in the grammar.** Gm, D, H, S. Gm makes it the most-important
miss; S lists it (K: "no mention of embodied experience, affective tone, sensory context"); D proposes a Materiality
slot (medium/tone/register) as its fix; H proposes a tonal/affective coding track. *(G touches it only obliquely, under
"loss," AC.)*

### MAJORITY (3/5)

**M1 — Neuro-symbolic auditability ≠ validity, and the grammar↔LLM mapping is not exposed.** G, Gm, S. "A fully
auditable analysis can be systematically reductive… the method emphasizes traceability more than construct validity"
(G); "black-box continuity… LLMs fill slots via surface pattern-matching while violating the grammar's constraints"
(Gm); "the concrete mapping between grammar and LM prompt is not exposed — auditability remains a promise" (S).
*(D and H treat neuro-symbolic as a strength.)*

**M2 — "Sitz" is boundless and/or circular — no rule of relevance for which conditions count.** G, D, H. "Without a
rule of relevance, situating becomes an indefinitely expanding contextual encyclopedia" (G, boundless); "the conditions
are evidenced from the text — a closed loop that silently relies on the coder's training data" (D, circular);
"could expand infinitely: biography, moment, medium, audience, institution… what counts?" (H, boundless). *(Gm, S out.)*

**M3 — Temporal / oral / performative dynamics are flattened; reception assumes a chronological arrow.** Gm, D, S.
"Spatializes and synchronizes what is diachronic, performative, or aurally experienced" (Gm); "Reception assumes linear
temporality… traditions work anachronically" (D); S proposes Temporal-Depth edge types (predecessor / successor /
re-interpretation) as the fix. *(G, H raise diachrony only as a proposed output, not a gap.)*

### SOLO / MINORITY (1–2/5)

- **Translation is a conditioning variable, not just provenance** (G-V, Gm — 2/5; echoed as a proposed *output* by D
  "Translation Forensics / betrayal map" and H "translation diagnostics").
- **Stance tags (endorsed/invoked/subverted/denied) are too discrete** — texts inhabit paradigms unconsciously, alternate
  stance across passages, perform strategic ambiguity (G-I, H — 2/5).
- **"Meaning-making" itself is never defined** — conflates semantic / pragmatic / interpretation / significance / effect /
  reference / world-disclosure (G-A — SOLO; but see CRT §2, this is our systematic scan's "telos unstated").
- **Compare's "consensus spine" prioritizes commonality over dissonance** — a theological/philological bias, not a neutral
  merge (D — SOLO; = our systematic scan's "merge-locus assumption").
- **Ontology of "text" is under-specified** — manuscript variants, performed poem, oral saying, translation, composite
  (G-B — SOLO; = our systematic scan's "text-category authority").
- **Inter-textual resonance beyond explicit edges** — allusion/echo/symbolic inheritance is a network of resonances, not a
  graph of explicit relations (S's most-important miss — SOLO; connects to G-N whole-text absence).
- **No declared loss function** — every representation discards; the method should state what it intentionally loses
  (G-AC — SOLO).
- **Compositionality / whole alters parts** — bottom-up composition modeled more clearly than top-down constraint (G-C —
  SOLO).
- **No output-specific evaluation criteria** — how is a good Compare/Gloss/Fan/Morphospace determined? (G-AD — SOLO).
- **Genre not structurally integrated** — the same sentence performs a different operation in lament vs law vs prayer
  (G-W — SOLO).

## 2. The CRT comparison — the "so what"

The reflexive test has three arms: (i) our **systematic 6-question scan** → 151 within-move gaps; (ii) the in-house
**3-agent free-form CRT** (three Claude agents, blind); (iii) this round, the **5-frontier-LLM** control — the
thesis-grade version of arm (ii). The comparison is the finding.

### (a) What BOTH the systematic scan and the free-form cohort found

The systematic scan's structural findings each show up somewhere in the free-form cohort — but as *scattered method-level
observations*, not as a scored per-unit scan:

| Systematic-scan finding | Free-form cohort echo |
|---|---|
| The telos / "what is meaning" unstated | G-A ("meaning-making never defined") |
| The "text" category authority (what counts as the input) | G-B ("ontology of a text under-specified") |
| The merge-locus assumption (Compare's spine) | D ("Compare presumes a consensus spine… a bias, not a neutral operation") |

So it is **not** "free-form missed the structural gaps." Individual frontier reviewers re-derived several of them
unprompted. What free-form could NOT do is deliver them **reproducibly, comprehensively, and scored per move × role** —
each cohort caught a different subset, none exhaustive; only the systematic scan produces the complete, comparable,
re-runnable coverage.

### (b) The meta-validity class the systematic scan STRUCTURALLY CANNOT surface — and the cohort surfaced it, hard

This is the crux. Our 6-question scan interrogates *completeness within the coded text* (is this move's negative space
scored, is this role filled). It has no question that can point back at the method's **own foundations**. The free-form
frontier cohort raised exactly that class — and converged on it more strongly than on anything else:

| Meta-validity critique (unavailable to our scan) | Cohort strength |
|---|---|
| Multi-LLM IRR ≠ validity; models aren't independent raters | **UNANIMOUS 5/5** (U1) |
| Gap/negative-space is unbounded → measures the model's priors, not the text | **UNANIMOUS 5/5** (U2) |
| The 5-slot grammar is an a-priori ontology (agentive/propositional/Western bias) | **STRONG-MAJORITY 4/5** (SM2) |
| No reader/reception; the coder's constitutive role is invisible | **STRONG-MAJORITY 4/5** (SM3) |
| Domain-generality non-falsifiable; self-application is circular | **STRONG-MAJORITY 4/5** (SM1) |
| Auditability = form-validity, not truth-validity | **MAJORITY 3/5** (M1) |

Every one of these matches a critique from the in-house 3-agent CRT — the frontier cohort **replicates the CRT's 10
convergent critiques** at thesis grade, and pushes two of them to full unanimity. The 3-agent CRT already argued the
structural point ("the method's own gap-scan structurally cannot critique its own foundations — that needs external /
free-form review"); IRR224 is the independent, higher-N confirmation.

### The verdict

**Yes — the 5LLM found what our systematic scan could not.** The scan and the free-form review are *complementary, not
competitive*: the scan owns reproducible completeness *within* the coded text; external free-form review owns critique of
the *foundations* of the coding. Neither can do the other's job. This is the direct argument for running the reflexive
gloss with a real external cohort rather than trusting the self-scan — the round validates its own methodology by
demonstrating the blind spot it was built to expose.

### The reflexive sting (put this in the thesis)

The round's single **unanimous** critique (U1) is that *multi-LLM agreement does not establish validity*. IRR224 **is**
a multi-LLM IRR round. So five independent frontier models **agreed** that "five independent models agreeing proves
nothing about truth." The consensus is self-undermining in exactly the productive way §2.8 predicts: it makes the
method's own bias visible rather than filtering it out. The honest reading is not "therefore IRR is worthless" but
"therefore IRR needs a human-expert gold anchor + an inter-model error-correlation discount + separated
segmentation-vs-label reliability" — the fix the 3-agent CRT already named (3/3), now backed 5/5.

### (c) What our scan found that the cohort missed

The systematic scan's *granular* deliverable — a specific unstated role for a specific move instance, scored across all
151 — is invisible to free-form. The cohort operates at the method-statement altitude (claims 1–13) and cannot descend
to move-instance completeness; no cohort produced anything resembling a per-move × per-role scored inventory. That
reproducible, comparable, re-runnable coverage is the systematic scan's unique and non-substitutable contribution.

## 3. Generative harvest

Convergent and high-leverage suggestions across the five (deduped against the CRT's generative gold).

### Top 3 (highest convergence × thesis leverage × buildability)

1. **Structural uncertainty → a Representation Ledger (the coded object as a versioned lattice, not a settled graph).**
   GPT's most developed move, converging with H (entangle provenance), S (XAI justification per gap; conflict-resolution
   log), D (positionality preamble). Every annotation stores: evidence span · annotation status (explicit / inferred /
   historically reconstructed / generated / disputed) · calibrated confidence · provenance · **alternative codings** ·
   loss note · challenge-condition. Downstream, a morphospace *point* becomes a confidence *region*; a Compare spine
   becomes a set of possible alignments. This directly answers the U-class (auditability≠validity, false neutrality,
   IRR-correlation) and is buildable on the existing store of record.

2. **Fan-as-validity-probe / intervention-based validation.** Converges G (intervention-based validation: alter voice /
   remove a warrant / reverse agency, then test whether predicted downstream effects occur), Gm (adversarial glossing:
   flip endorsed→subverted, trace the semantic collapse to expose load-bearing pillars), D ("Generative Contradiction":
   generate maximally distinct anti-moves per gap, measure the tradition's capacity to absorb them) — and the CRT (2/3).
   Perturb one slot and check the coding changes *coherently in the predicted slot*: this tests that the coder tracks
   the **text**, not its priors, and gives the non-falsifiable domain-generality claim (SM1) a real falsification. High
   leverage, low cost — it re-purposes an output the instrument already has.

3. **Pluralize the grammar → a "grammar of grammars," with a residual + silence typology.** Converges G (plural
   coexisting ontologies + a translation layer among partial grammars; emic/indigenous ontologies where mapping fails =
   the discovery), H (tradition-endogenous validation against actual exegetes), Gm (topological hole-mining: aggregate
   negative spaces → a map of a tradition's shared silences / taboos), S (dynamic self-organizing morphospace axes),
   and the CRT (grammar-of-grammars + silence typology + residual atlas). Allow tradition-native (non-agentive,
   apophatic) frames and make the *mapping-failure between grammars* the object of study; log a per-move "does-not-fit"
   residual channel → a **residual atlas** and a **comparative apophatics engine**. This is the direct structural answer
   to SM2 (the agentive-ontology critique) and the strongest available novelty claim.

### The rest, grouped

- **Disagreement-as-signal** (keep the inter-model disagreement map as a feature, not noise): G interpretive-instability
  map + disagreement atlas; S Edge-Stability Index; matches CRT. High-disagreement moves = the tradition's live
  controversies.
- **Translation stress-test / forensics** (run source + N translations, overlay move-structures, highlight agent/outcome
  drift): G, D ("betrayal map"), H — 3/5 propose it independently.
- **Diachronic reception / hermeneutic genealogy** (stack the coded object across historical readings; how a provisional
  gap-fill becomes institutionalized as obvious): G, H (diachronic reception mapping), S (Morphospace Trajectory
  Tracker), D (Reception Radar) — 4/5.
- **Human-expert / tradition-bearer anchor** for validation (reciprocal critique of the *categories*, not just codings):
  G-U, H (tradition-endogenous validation), D — matches the CRT human gold set.
- **Benchmark of hard cases / "show me a failure"** (a corpus built to break the method — pun-poem, apophatic text,
  mantra, visual poem, manuscript-variant text; publish the failures): G novelty-K, and H makes "show me a failure case"
  its cross-check question. Matches CRT residual atlas.
- **Missing-voice / topological silence audit** (agents present in the text's world but denied speech/interiority/warrant):
  G, Gm — operationalizes ideological silence without assuming every absence is an error.
- **Gap-driven generation** (use high-score gaps as prompts to generate speculative continuations; test whether blind
  recoders recover the intended structure — a closed experimental loop): G generative-composition assistant, S, Gm.
- **Cross-modal / materiality extension** (substrate as a multimodal slot — image, score, performance): S, D.
- **Recursive self-revision** (encode method → detect gaps → generate revisions → rerun → track which problems persist):
  G, Gm ("Ouroboros"), H (recursive meta-moves).

## 4. §13 citation-veracity flags

**Round verdict: CLEAN. No cohort fabricated a scholarly citation.** This was a judgment/review task (no
citation-retrieval was requested), so the fabrication surface is small by design — which is exactly why the round was
built as a judgment round.

- **GPT-5, Gemini, DeepSeek, Haiku** — cite no named works or scholars. They use generic disciplinary concepts
  (compositionality, question-under-discussion, apophatic discourse, memetic/phylogenetic) and adjectival
  tradition-labels (Talmudic, Sufi, Zen, Derridean, postcolonial). None is a source-claim; nothing to flag. (Gemini
  typos "substrates" as "subrates" — cosmetic, not a veracity issue.)
- **Solar — specifically noted per feedback_solar_judgment_not_retrieval_cohort.** Solar was correctly included as a
  judgment cohort and performed as one: a 12-row gap table, a Resonance-Layer proposal, 7 outputs, 7 novelty ideas — all
  substantive, none resting on an invented source. Its only fingerprint is that its *illustrative example* research-
  questions skew heavily Korean (Gyeonggi folk-song, Samguk Sagi, Jangseung, Korean pop lyrics) — an artifact of Solar
  being an Upstage/Korean-built model. These are proposed hypothetical use-cases, **not** citations presented as real
  scholarship, so they are **not** §13 violations. Net: **Solar reliable for this task; the Solar-IN-for-judgment rule
  is affirmed.**
