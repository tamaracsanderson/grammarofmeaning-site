# Two kinds of gap, side by side

Both columns reviewed the **same object** — the instrument's own method-statement (`REFLEXIVE-METHOD`, coded into 38 moves).
The point of this page is to let you SEE the difference between what the two review styles produce, in their own words.

- **LEFT** = our **coded process** — the systematic scan. 151 gaps, each one attached to a specific move, tagged by which
  role/slot is unsaid, scored, and dispositioned (filled-internally / residual / barred). Distribution across the 151:
  `reason 38 · warrant 35 · instrument 29 · edge 26 · degree 16 · reciprocity 5 · interior 2`; `126 residual · 25 filled-internally`.
- **RIGHT** = **free-form review** — 5 frontier LLMs (IRR224: GPT-5 · Gemini 2.5 Pro · DeepSeek · Haiku · Solar) + the
  in-house 3-agent CRT, each reading the method-statement blind, no scaffold, no sight of the 151.

## OVERLAP — what BOTH found (same site, different depth)

Three of the scan's structural findings were re-derived, unprompted, by individual free-form reviewers. The two styles
are not blind to each other — they hit some of the same sites. But the coded scan hit them as *scored, located* gaps;
free-form hit them as *scattered method-level observations*:

| The shared site | LEFT (located + scored) | RIGHT (named at method-altitude) |
|---|---|---|
| **The telos / "what is meaning"** | M1 · reason · 0.85 (telos withheld) + M11 · warrant · 0.95 | GPT-5: *"meaning-making never defined"* |
| **What counts as a "text"** | M4 · warrant · 0.92 (authority for collapsing scripture+lyric+methodology) | GPT-5: *"ontology of a text under-specified"* |
| **Compare's merge-locus** | M19→M20 · edge · 0.91 (why move-graph merge is licensed) | DeepSeek: *"Compare presumes a consensus spine… a bias"* |

The sharpest overlap is **M11**: the coded scan flagged an *unfilled warrant slot* — "on whose authority is the
five-slot ontology valid" — and the free-form cohort walked through that exact door and named what's behind it: the
grammar *is* an a-priori ontology that "may manufacture the cross-tradition convergence it claims." Same site. The scan
finds the hole; the free-form review names the danger in the hole.

## DIVERGENCE — what each found that the other structurally could not

| LEFT found (free-form structurally could NOT) | RIGHT found (the scan structurally could NOT) |
|---|---|
| A **complete, per-move × per-role, scored inventory** — all 151 gaps, comparable and re-runnable. E.g. the distribution `reason 38 · warrant 35 · instrument 29 · …` is itself a finding: the method *says WHAT it does clearly but under-justifies WHY, ON-WHOSE-AUTHORITY, and BY-WHAT-MEANS*. Free-form produced nothing resembling a per-move scored table — "each cohort caught a different subset, none exhaustive" (IRR224 §2a). | A **critique of the method's own foundations** — the meta-validity class: *IRR ≠ validity* (5/5), *gaps unbounded* (5/5), *grammar-is-ontology* (4/5), *no-reader* (4/5). "Our 6-question scan interrogates completeness *within* the coded text… It has no question that can point back at the method's own foundations" (IRR224 §2b). |
| **Disposition tracking** — which gaps the text answers itself (25 filled-internally) vs. which are handed to reception (126 residual). A structural property of *this text*, invisible to a foundations critique. | **The reflexive sting** — five independent models unanimously agreed that "five independent models agreeing proves nothing about truth" (U1). The scan cannot turn on its own warrant this way; a foundations critique can. |

The reason for the divergence is structural, not a matter of effort: the scan's six questions ask *"is this move's
negative space scored, is this role filled?"* — every one points *into* the coded text. None points *back at the frame*.
So the scan is constitutionally incapable of asking whether the frame itself is sound — which is precisely the class the
free-form cohort converged on hardest.

---

## So what (3 sentences)

The two are **complementary, not competitive**: the coded process owns *reproducible within-text completeness* — a
scored, comparable, re-runnable inventory of every unsaid role at every move, which no free-form reviewer produced or
could produce — while free-form review owns *critique of the foundations* — whether the grammar is a neutral scaffold or
an imposed ontology, whether multi-LLM agreement is validity, whether meaning lives in a reader the method never codes.
Neither can do the other's job: the scan cannot point back at its own frame (all six of its questions point inward), and
the cohort cannot descend to per-move-instance completeness (it operates at the method-statement altitude and each
reviewer catches only a partial subset). The honest reading of the reflexive test is therefore that the instrument needs
**both arms** — the scan for defensible coverage, the external cohort for defensible foundations — and running only the
self-scan would leave exactly the blind spot the cohort was built to expose.
