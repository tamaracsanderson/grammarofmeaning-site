Where v1 rendered the coded move-edges, v2 renders the sound: every arc links two words that rhyme, share a stressed vowel (assonance), share an onset (alliteration), or share a consonant skeleton (consonance) — computed from the real text with pronouncing (CMU phones) + panphon (articulatory feature distance). Nothing is invented; filter by an ear. The layer a poem composes in: the ear's resemblance-chains that bind distant words (here me/Immortality, away/Day, Ground/Ground) — computed, not invented.
