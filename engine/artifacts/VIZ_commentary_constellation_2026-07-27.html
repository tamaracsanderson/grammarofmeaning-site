Two kinds of reader see different silences

A coded passage's open gaps are read by a panel of corpus-personas (real scholars, seated on their own texts) and atlas-frames (stance-coordinates). They nominate the gaps their stance notices; a chair selects a few; then everyone writes a fill. The two reader-types nominate non-overlapping gaps — and which axis each owns flips by domain.

The interior position swaps owners

Same instrument, same “felt-from-inside” gap-position. The reader-type that owns it reverses when the personas change — because a Freudian and an Augustinian genuinely read for different things. The frames stay constant; the personas rotate the axis.
