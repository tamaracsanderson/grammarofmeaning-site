RESITUATE — the sensitivity analysis

Perturb one documented Sitz lever, regenerate in three independent rooms, and see which of the text's moves survive. Levers that reorganize plausibly in all three rooms are robust — structural to the text. Levers that bend are contingent on their world. The multi-lever floor test should collapse — and it does, which is the proof the method discriminates.
