How the instrument works

One text goes in. Two engines read it at once, at different grains — one takes the whole text’s bearings, the other reads it clause by clause — and their readings meet in one coded object, from which five recompositions fan. Read it as an aerial map: fly over first, then click into the detail where you need it. Each stage is an experiment — what are we testing, and did it hold?

The method, named — this is the rigor
Controlled experiment · ablation
Hold everything constant, change one variable, observe the effect (ceteris paribus). The single-lever perturbation — the fan, RESITUATE — is an ablation.
Mill’s Method of Difference
Two cases differing in outcome and in exactly one circumstance → that circumstance is the cause (J.S. Mill, 1843). The 325-vs-381 creed comparison, precisely.
Counterfactual vs a natural experiment
“Change the 325 variable to match 381, see if it reproduces the outcome” — the counterfactual validated against history’s real manipulation, with the attested text as ground truth.

The whole = the comparative method applied to historical forks — a causal-inference method for how meaning changes. Honest caveat: a single case is comparative-historical, not statistical; it becomes a genuine across-case comparison as forks accumulate (Decision 202).

Outputs — the recomposition, three ways

Recomposition is not one thing. The instrument produces three kinds of output, and each wears its epistemic status before it is read. These are the product the whole instrument makes — not tests of a mechanism. One lane runs today; two are design sketches.

The one lane that runs today — the SUBSTITUTE pipeline
→→→

A pipeline, not a branch: each step feeds the next. The three engines below it (FILL, BEQUEATH, COMPARE) are design sketches — specified, not built.

On “rhyme.” When two traditions echo, we report it as a rhyme — the same move in a different voice — not as independent convergence. A correspondence is an analyst’s claim, never a native connection between the traditions; the CITES edge exists precisely to disqualify a borrowed pair from counting.

The output already working: 325 → 381, a predictive-validity result

The clearest end-to-end run to date is a real historical fork. RESITUATE registered two predictions about how the Nicene creed of 325 would be revised, before the revised 381 creed was coded. Comparing the two, both held — and the East–West filioque schism is one move away (a single ‘and the Son’ edit to 381’s procession clause). This is Mill’s Method of Difference applied to history: two cases, one differing circumstance, the attested outcome as ground truth. The fan today holds 37 alternate-world trees.

Evidence — how the method is tested

The engines make claims about form. A claim about form has to be tested the way you test an instrument: out of its home domain, with the prediction written down first, by more than one independent reader. Four methods do that; each has run on real material. Below the methods, the lab notebook — the one-page report behind each result.

How we test the method

The engines make claims about form — that a text’s frame, its moves, and its spine can be read the same way across traditions and genres. A claim about form has to be tested the way you test an instrument: out of its home domain, with the answer written down in advance, and by more than one independent reader. Four methods do that, and each has run on real material.

methodwhat it checksthe tellrun so far
Clean-Room Triangulation
Decision 200		 Is a load-bearing design right? Three isolated rooms build (or judge) the same thing with no contact. convergence = confidence; pooled flags = the gaps (one room catching a missing field is enough). atlas frame-reader (3 rooms → one schema; caught a missing enacted-vs-attributed field); Tender Buttons (unanimous PASS).
Attested-fork / counterfactual
Decision 202		 Does the counterfactual PREDICT the attested revision? Register the prediction, then code the real fork. Mill’s Method of Difference — two cases, one differing circumstance is the cause; the attested outcome is ground truth. 325 vs 381: both pre-registered predictions held (anathemas vanish; Spirit clause strengthens).
Out-of-domain replication
§2.14 · IRR167		 Is a result a property of the instrument or the domain? Re-run on a confound-free secular corpus. a result that survives the domain change is the instrument’s; one that doesn’t is the domain’s. A replication that fails is a finding. Romans’ present-vs-obligated reliability split reproduced on a Beatles lyric, 5/5.
Morphospace scoring
the atlas		 Which axes carry signal, and are they independent? Pure computation on the coded coordinates. Cramér’s V (axis independence) + per-axis entropy + edge-density as a genre fingerprint. edges/move already stratifies by genre: Creed 0.87 › narrative 0.31–0.59 › Stein 0.08.
The two principles underneath. Test out of domain (§2.14): religion is the proof-of-concept, not the scope — so the instrument is stressed on a pop lyric, on Stein’s stream-of-consciousness, on a historical schism. Make the bias visible (§2.8): pre-register the prediction, cast wide, report what was excluded and why. A limit shown is stronger than a limit hidden.

Audit — schema, revisions, failures

Reader mode keeps the explanation clean. The audit material — every field’s definition, its controlled vocabulary, its recorded caveats — lives inside each stage panel and is revealed by the Audit toggle in the nav (or “Explore the method in detail” inside a panel). Nothing is hidden; it is layered so the audit never overwhelms the read.

The status vocabulary — one eight-state scale, encoded by icon + pattern (never colour alone)
●LIVEruns on the corpusin use
◐PILOTbuilt, thin coveragein use
▨PROVISIONALrunning but mid-revision / not settledreserved
◇DESIGNEDspecified, not builtin use
▤LEGACYcoded on a since-retired instrumentreserved
○DORMANTin the schema, never runreserved
✕FAILEDtried, did not holdreserved
?UNRESOLVEDa data-integrity flag to reconcilereserved

A node’s status is visible before you read its content (crit IX.2). Today the stages occupy three of the eight states; the rest are reserved so a stage can be honestly marked LEGACY, FAILED or UNRESOLVED when a test says so, rather than quietly staying “green.”
